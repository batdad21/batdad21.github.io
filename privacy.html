Claude Batlab privacy policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026.

Claude Batlab is used by its operator to process work meeting recordings stored in Google Drive and create draft summaries. It is not offered as a public service.

The app reads meeting recordings and file details from Drive. It keeps draft transcripts and summaries in the operator's private workspace and places generated summary documents in Drive. Transcript text is shared with a third-party AI service to draft summaries. Data is not sold or used for advertising.

The Google account owner controls the source files and generated documents in Drive and may revoke the app's access through their Google account. To request deletion of data held by the operator or ask about this policy, email firephoenix21@gmail.com.
