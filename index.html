Claude Batlab

Claude Batlab is a private automation used by its operator to turn work meeting recordings in a dedicated Google Drive folder into draft transcripts, summaries, and a meeting log.

The app requests Google Drive access so it can read recordings and maintain summary documents in that folder. It is not offered as a public service.

For details about data handling, read the privacy policy. Questions about the app can be sent to firephoenix21@gmail.com.
